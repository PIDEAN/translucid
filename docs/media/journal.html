Write in the language you think in

By the Notara team · 5 min read

Most of us switch languages all day without noticing. We read documentation in one, chat with colleagues in another, and take notes in whatever comes first. The tools we use rarely make that easy.

When we started Notara, we wanted an editor that gets out of the way. That meant fewer buttons, quieter colors and an interface that feels familiar on the first day.

Small words carry the most weight

A settings page is full of short labels: Small, Medium, Large. Out of context they are easy to translate and easy to get wrong, because the same word can answer very different questions.

Good localization keeps the question next to the answer. It treats a group of options as one unit, so each choice reads naturally in the target language.

Keep what is yours

Your notes, names and titles belong to you. A translation layer should change the interface around them and leave your own words exactly as you wrote them.
